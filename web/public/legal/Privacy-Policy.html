1. Scope and Legal Basis

This Privacy Policy is issued under the Personal Data Protection Act 2010 (Act 709) of Malaysia, as amended by the Personal Data Protection (Amendment) Act 2024 (Act A1727), and governs the processing of personal data by [Company Name] Sdn. Bhd. (“we”, “us”) in providing our inference services.

This Policy is read together with our Data Policy, which describes processing at the technical level. Where the two differ as to content data, the Data Policy prevails.

We are a data controller established in Malaysia and the PDPA applies to our processing of personal data in connection with the Service, including the transfer of personal data to Thailand under Section 129. For certain processing activities we apply standards more stringent than the law requires (for example, retaining no request content), which does not affect the applicability of the PDPA as stated above.

2. Our Role

We are a private limited company incorporated in Malaysia, named Maknaa Group Sdn. Bhd., company registration number 202501037327 (1638737-V), registered at Kuala Lumpur.

For the metering and technical metadata generated by inference requests we determine the purposes and periods of retention ourselves and act as a data controller under the PDPA. Where an enterprise customer requires us to act as its processor for particular data, the parties enter into a separate data processing agreement (DPA).

12. Cross-border Transfer

We are established as a data controller in Malaysia and our inference capacity is located in Thailand, so personal data is processed outside Malaysia.

Under Section 129 of the PDPA, an overseas transfer requires one of the following: the destination has a law substantially similar to the Act or ensures at least an equivalent level of protection (s.129(2)); or a ground under s.129(3) applies, including your explicit consent, necessity for performance of a contract with you, necessity for a contract with a third party entered into at your request or in your interest, legal proceedings or exercise of legal rights, reasonable grounds to avoid or mitigate harm to you, having taken all reasonable precautions and exercised all due diligence, or protection of your vital interests.

Our approach: a transfer impact assessment (TIA) has been completed for Thailand, concluding that protection is not lower than the PDPA requires, under s.129(2)(b); it is valid for three years and is repeated on expiry or material change of law; supplementary measures apply (encryption in transit, access control, data minimisation, no content retention); and written agreements are in place with overseas processors.

For data subjects in the European Union, any transfer from the EU to Malaysia or Thailand relies on the European Commission’s Standard Contractual Clauses (Decision 2021/914) with a transfer impact assessment and supplementary measures.

Enterprise customers that transfer personal data outside Malaysia on their own account must complete their own assessment of that transfer and retain a record of it.

13. Personal Data Breach Notification

Under Section 12B of the PDPA (in force 1 June 2025), where we have reason to believe a personal data breach has occurred, we notify the Personal Data Protection Commissioner as soon as practicable and no later than 72 hours after becoming aware, stating the nature of the breach, the categories and number of data subjects affected, the likely consequences and the measures taken.

Where the breach is likely to cause significant harm to individuals, we notify affected individuals within 7 days after notifying the Commissioner, describing the breach, its likely consequences and recommended protective steps.

We maintain a personal data breach register recording all incidents, including those below the notification threshold, retained for at least two years.
